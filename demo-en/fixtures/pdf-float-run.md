The prose shares the same band as the picture: the image sits on the right and the text wraps on the left. With shared float space off, the picture and this prose are treated as one unit, so whichever does not fit moves with the other. With it on, the prose may stay on this page and only the picture moves. This paragraph is deliberately long enough to create a wrap band at the page edge, ending at this point.
